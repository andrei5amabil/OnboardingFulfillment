# POL-OFF: Offboarding Compliance & Data Retention Guidelines

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy establishes mandatory operational protocols, execution sequencing, and data retention standards for processing departing personnel across all corporate infrastructure, identity domains, and asset repositories. It enforces a deterministic, zero-trust offboarding lifecycle designed to systematically eliminate orphaned identities, reclaim enterprise licensing capital, prevent post-employment data exfiltration, ensure business continuity through asset handover, and produce tamper-evident audit records for regulatory compliance.

## 2. Immediate Credential Invalidation & Session Revocation
- **[POL-OFF-201] Deterministic Session Termination:** Upon reaching the official termination timestamp or upon execution of an emergency termination order, the execution engine must immediately transmit an atomic revocation broadcast across the Central Directory Service and federated Identity Provider, terminating all active single sign-on (SSO) sessions, refresh tokens, and active web sessions across all enterprise environments.
- **[POL-OFF-202] Credential Scrambling & Directory Deactivation:** The Central Directory Service must immediately lock the employee account, randomize authentication password hashes, disable interactive directory logon, and transition the identity object into a dedicated Decommissioned organizational unit.
- **[POL-OFF-203] Multi-Factor Authentication Invalidation:** All enrolled multi-factor authentication (MFA) devices, cryptographic hardware tokens, mobile authenticator bindings, and registered biometric factors associated with the departing employee must be permanently revoked to prevent subsequent authentication re-establishment.
- **[POL-OFF-204] Automated SaaS Entitlement Reclaiming:** The deterministic orchestration engine must automatically revoke all assigned software-as-a-service application seats, enterprise tool profiles, and security group memberships, returning the reclaimed licenses to the unallocated organizational inventory pools in the database.
- **[POL-OFF-205] Scheduled vs. Immediate Execution Protocols:** Standard scheduled departures must execute deterministic credential revocation precisely at 17:00 local campus time on the employee’s final working day. High-risk or immediate terminations flagged by HR or Corporate Security must trigger instantaneous revocation within 60 seconds of payload confirmation.

## 3. Work Continuity & Digital Asset Transfer
- **[POL-OFF-301] Mandatory Digital Work Handover:** Prior to account deactivation, the offboarding workflow must execute an automated asset reassignment routine, transferring primary administrative ownership of active code repositories, shared cloud storage drives, operational document folders, and assigned workflow tickets to the direct supervisor or designated successor specified in the payload (`transfer_ownership_to`).
- **[POL-OFF-302] Shared Secrets & Service Account Rotation:** Any private cryptographic keys, API access tokens, cloud service credentials, or administrative secrets checked out or generated by the departing employee must be marked as compromised and queued for immediate automated rotation in the enterprise secrets vault.
- **[POL-OFF-303] Messaging and Mailbox Redirection Protocol:** Outbound and inbound electronic mail communications for the departing identity must be configured with a standardized automated corporate out-of-office notification directing inquiries to the departmental manager. Forwarding rules to non-corporate, external personal email addresses are strictly prohibited and blocked at the transport layer.
- **[POL-OFF-304] Enterprise Collaboration Channel Scrubbing:** The orchestration engine must remove the departing user from all internal distribution lists, private project channels, customer-facing groups, and privileged messaging channels.

## 4. Hardware Recovery & Physical Asset Sanitization
- **[POL-OFF-401] Automated Waybill Generation & Logistics:** The execution engine must extract all active computing equipment and physical peripherals registered to the employee in the IT asset database and automatically dispatch prepaid, insured courier shipping waybills and packaging instructions to the employee's verified physical address.
- **[POL-OFF-402] Remote Device Locking via Endpoint Management:** At the scheduled termination time, the Unified Endpoint Management (UEM) system must dispatch a remote lock command to all assigned laptops and mobile endpoints, disabling local interactive account login and restricting the device to firmware-level cryptographic protection while in transit.
- **[POL-OFF-403] Physical Surrender Window & Asset Return:** Terminated personnel are required to dispatch and surrender all assigned physical IT equipment within 5 business days following their effective termination date.
- **[POL-OFF-404] Cryptographic Data Purge Standards:** Upon physical arrival at the central hardware depot, IT technicians must perform an automated cryptographic data wipe adhering to enterprise data sanitization baselines, verifying that all storage sectors are cryptographically erased before the asset can be re-imaged or returned to general inventory.
- **[POL-OFF-405] Unrecovered Hardware Escalation:** If equipment return tracking is not initiated within 10 business days of departure, the asset inventory system must flag the device serial numbers as missing, trigger a remote cryptographic kill switch, and submit a security incident report for legal and financial remediation.

## 5. Retention Holds, Quarantine Buffers & Purging
- **[POL-OFF-501] Encrypted 90-Day Retention Hold:** Deactivated accounts, email archives, and user workspace storage must remain in an encrypted, non-interactive quarantine state for an exact duration of 90 calendar days to preserve operational continuity and accommodate regulatory audit requests.
- **[POL-OFF-502] Legal and Compliance Hold Precedence:** If an active legal hold or regulatory inquiry is flagged against the departing employee prior to or during the 90-day quarantine period, all automated deletion and data purge routines must be indefinitely suspended until explicit written release from the enterprise compliance officer.
- **[POL-OFF-503] Irreversible Data Destruction:** Following the completion of the mandatory 90-day quarantine period (in the absence of an active legal hold), the data lifecycle engine must execute an automated, irreversible cryptographic deletion of the archived mailbox, personal directory spaces, and authentication records.

## 6. Verification, Reconciliation & Audit Sign-Off
- **[POL-OFF-601] Zero Residual Access Multi-System Audit:** Before offboarding sign-off can be granted, the verification runner must execute cross-system reconciliation queries across the Central Directory Service, identity gateways, SaaS tenant management APIs, and network firewalls to verify zero active entitlements, zero active tokens, and zero remaining directory memberships.
- **[POL-OFF-602] Orphaned Entitlement Alerting & Process Halting:** If the multi-system reconciliation scan detects any residual active account, lingering SaaS seat assignment, or unrevoked network route, the offboarding workflow must immediately halt, mark the run as non-compliant, and dispatch a critical alert to the IT Systems Administrator.
- **[POL-OFF-603] Immutable Audit Trail Persistence:** Upon verified achievement of zero residual access, the execution runner must compile an authoritative compliance report containing high-resolution event timestamps, executing agent IDs, revoked license keys, asset tracking numbers, and handover recipient identifiers, permanently persisting the record in the transactional database `audit_logs` table.
